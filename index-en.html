Welcome to XIAMEN HUNGJIN TRADING CO.,LTD!

Xiamen HungJin Trade Company was first established in Xiamen City, Fujian Province, in 2006, which specialized in knitted fabric for sportswear, swimsuit, active wear, and underwear, etc. We have our own factory to yarn, knit, dye, print and special finishing treatment for all kinds of different knitted fabric including warp knit and circular knit. We're driven to create high quality fabric that is functional, technical, fashionable and exceptional value.

HungJin strives for long-term relationships with our clients. Most of all, we look forward to serving you with passion and excellence. On behalf of every employee at HungJin, we appreciate your support over the past years. Welcome to HungJin, Welcome to China.
