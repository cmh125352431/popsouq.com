What is 4-Way Stretch Fabric?

Published: 2026-01-01Source: Xiamen Hungjin Trading

What is 4-Way Stretch Fabric?

4-way stretch fabric refers to fabrics that have good elasticity in both the warp (longitudinal) and weft (transverse) directions. This fabric can stretch and recover freely in all four directions, providing unrestricted movement during wear, hence the name "4-way stretch."

Characteristics of 4-Way Stretch Fabric

  • 4-Way Elasticity: Elastic in both warp and weft directions, comfortable and unrestricted
  • Good Recovery: Quickly returns to original shape after stretching, resistant to deformation
  • Body-Hugging: Fabric stretches freely with body movement, conforming to body curves
  • Comfortable Wear: Freedom of movement, suitable for sports and daily wear
  • Wide Applicability: Can be used for various types of clothing

Classification of 4-Way Stretch Fabrics

  • Polyester 4-Way Stretch: Polyester and spandex blend, crisp and washable
  • Nylon 4-Way Stretch: Nylon and spandex blend, soft and abrasion-resistant
  • Cotton 4-Way Stretch: Cotton and spandex blend, breathable and comfortable

Applications of 4-Way Stretch Fabric

Due to its excellent elasticity, 4-way stretch fabric is widely used in:

  • Yoga wear, fitness wear
  • Dancewear, gymnastics wear
  • Sports jackets, windbreakers
  • Casual pants, jeans
  • Performance costumes, theatrical costumes
  • Medical rehabilitation clothing

How to Choose 4-Way Stretch Fabric

  • Check elasticity: Quality 4-way stretch recovers quickly after stretching
  • Check composition: Higher spandex content means better elasticity (typically 5%-30%)
  • Check recovery: Test by repeated stretching to see if it deforms
  • Check intended use: Different uses require different composition ratios

4-way stretch fabric is the ideal choice for modern elastic clothing. Choosing the right fabric makes garments more comfortable to wear and allows for greater freedom of movement.
