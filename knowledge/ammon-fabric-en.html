What is Spandex Fabric?

Published: 2026-01-01 Source: Xiamen Hungjin Trading

What is Spandex?

Spandex is a synthetic fiber composed primarily of polyurethane, known for its exceptional elasticity. Its chemical name is "polyurethane fiber," and the name "Spandex" is an anagram of "expands," perfectly reflecting its defining characteristic — extreme stretchability.

Characteristics of Spandex

  • Outstanding Elasticity: Spandex can stretch to 5-8 times its original length and has excellent recovery, returning to its original shape quickly after stretching.
  • Chemical Resistance: Good resistance to most chemicals, including sweat, seawater, and oils.
  • Aging Resistance: Resistant to degradation, ensuring a long service life.
  • Comfort: Soft and form-fitting, providing a comfortable, restriction-free wearing experience.
  • Easy Dyeing: Can be dyed with various dyes, producing vibrant and long-lasting colors.

Spandex in Fabric Applications

Spandex is rarely used alone. It is typically blended with other fibers (such as polyester, nylon, or cotton) to create various elastic fabrics. Common spandex-blend fabrics include:

  • Polyester-Spandex Blends: Combining the crispness of polyester with the elasticity of spandex.
  • Nylon-Spandex Blends: Offering a soft hand feel with excellent wear resistance.
  • Cotton-Spandex Blends: Providing breathability and comfort with moderate stretch.

Application Areas

Thanks to its exceptional elasticity, spandex fabrics are widely used in:

  • Swimwear, sportswear, and activewear
  • Underwear and shapewear
  • Yoga wear and dancewear
  • Jeans and casual pants
  • Socks (especially compression socks)
  • Medical products (such as elastic bandages)

Care Tips for Spandex Fabrics

  • Wash in water below 30°C
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure to prevent degradation
  • Avoid vigorous rubbing during washing
  • Do not use chlorine bleach
  • Use low ironing temperatures

With its outstanding elasticity and comfort, spandex has become an indispensable component of modern apparel fabrics. Choosing the right spandex content and blend ratio can meet the functional requirements of different garments.
