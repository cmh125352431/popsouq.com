What is Spandex Fabric?

Published: 2026-01-01 Source: Xiamen Hungjin Trading

What is Spandex?

Spandex (also known as Lycra or elastane) is a synthetic fiber made primarily from polyurethane. It has exceptional elasticity. The name "Spandex" is derived from "span" (to stretch) and "expand," perfectly describing its characteristic - the ability to stretch up to 5-8 times its original length and recover quickly.

Characteristics of Spandex

  • Excellent Elasticity: Spandex can stretch 5-8 times its length with excellent recovery, returning to original shape immediately after stretching
  • Chemical Resistance: Good resistance to most chemicals, sweat, seawater, and oils
  • Aging Resistance: Does not age easily, ensuring long service life
  • Comfort: Soft and form-fitting, providing comfortable wear without restriction
  • Easy to Dye: Can be dyed with various dyes, producing vibrant and lasting colors

Applications of Spandex in Fabrics

Spandex is rarely used alone. It is typically blended with other fibers (such as polyester, nylon, cotton) to create various elastic fabrics. Common spandex-blended fabrics include:

  • Polyester Spandex: Combines polyester's crispness with spandex's elasticity
  • Nylon Spandex: Soft hand feel with excellent abrasion resistance
  • Cotton Spandex: Breathable and comfortable with moderate elasticity

Application Areas of Spandex Fabrics

Due to its excellent elasticity, spandex fabric is widely used in:

  • Swimwear, sportswear, fitness wear
  • Underwear, shapewear
  • Yoga wear, dancewear
  • Jeans, casual pants
  • Socks (especially compression socks)
  • Medical supplies (such as elastic bandages)

How to Care for Spandex Fabrics

  • Water temperature should not be too high, recommended below 30°C
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure to prevent aging
  • Avoid vigorous rubbing when washing
  • Do not use chlorine bleach
  • Ironing temperature should not be too high

With its excellent elasticity and comfort, spandex fabric has become an indispensable component in modern apparel fabrics. Choosing the right spandex content and blend ratio can meet the functional requirements of different garments.
