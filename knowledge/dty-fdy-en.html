What is the Difference Between DTY and FDY?

Published: 2026-01-01Source: Xiamen Hungjin Trading

Basic Concepts

DTY (Draw Textured Yarn): Textured yarn made by drawing and false-twist texturing POY

FDY (Fully Drawn Yarn): Fully drawn yarn made by direct drawing and orientation during spinning

Main Differences

FeatureDTYFDY
Production ProcessPOY + Drawing/TexturingOne-step Spinning/Drawing
ElasticityGood elasticity, crimpedLess elastic, more rigid
BulkinessGood bulkinessModerate bulkiness
Hand FeelSoftCrisp
StrengthMediumHigh
AppearanceSimilar to staple yarnSmooth and uniform
PriceLowerHigher

Application Areas

DTY is suitable for:

  • Elastic fabrics, spandex covered yarn
  • Knitted fabrics
  • Socks, underwear
  • Home textiles

FDY is suitable for:

  • Silk-like fabrics
  • Women's wear fabrics
  • Decorative fabrics
  • Industrial fabrics

How to Choose

The choice between DTY and FDY depends on the requirements of the final product:

  • Need good elasticity → Choose DTY
  • Need crisp hand feel → Choose FDY
  • Need bulky appearance → Choose DTY
  • Need high strength → Choose FDY

Both fibers have their own characteristics. In actual production, DTY and FDY can also be combined to achieve the best results.
