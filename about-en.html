Company Profile

Xiamen Hungjin Trading Co., Ltd. was established in 2009, located in the beautiful coastal city of Xiamen, Fujian Province. We specialize in the production and sales of warp-knitted and weft-knitted fabrics, integrating R&D, production, and trade.

Our main products include high-quality fabrics such as polyester/spandex, nylon/spandex, 4-way stretch, and microfiber, widely used in garments, swimwear, sportswear, underwear, footwear, and more.

Adhering to the philosophy of "Quality First, Customer First", we provide customers with superior products at reasonable prices and comprehensive services. Our products are sold well in domestic market and exported to Southeast Asia, Middle East, Europe, America, and other regions.
